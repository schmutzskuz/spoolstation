# Managing the library

The **Library** is your overview of all spools, filaments and manufacturers from Spoolman. Pick an entry on the left; see and edit the details on the right.

![The library](images/bibliothek.png)

## Search, filter, group

At the top of the list you can:

- **Search** across everything (name, material, manufacturer …).
- **Group** by filament or manufacturer – or show all spools without grouping.
- **Sort** by last used, name, remaining weight or registration date.
- **Filter** by material, manufacturer, location, with/without tag and archived spools.

## Editing a spool

Open a spool to see all its fields. Most values can be clicked and edited directly (name, location, price, notes …). Changes are saved to Spoolman immediately; **"Undo"** reverts the last change.

![Spool detail with weights and usage](images/spool.png)

### Measured

If you've put the spool on a scale, click **"Measured"** and enter the **gross weight** (spool with the core). Spoolman works out the remaining weight from that. This is the most accurate way to keep the remaining weight current.

### Usage

With **"Usage"** you subtract a specific number of grams, e.g. after a print you didn't log through the station.

### Archiving

**Archiving** hides an empty or old spool without deleting it. You can bring archived spools back via the filter. This keeps the list tidy without losing any data.

### Deleting

**Delete** removes the spool from Spoolman for good. If it was the last spool of a filament, the app asks whether to delete the filament as well. Tip: when in doubt, **archive** rather than delete.

## Filaments and manufacturers

Select a **filament** on the left to see its properties and all its spools. A **manufacturer** shows all of its filaments. These entries can be edited, duplicated and deleted too.

## Several spools at once

You can select several spools and **archive** or **delete** them together – handy for tidying up.
